Why Use a Multiplication Grid?

A multiplication grid lays out every product from 1×1 to 20×20 in one place, making it easy to spot patterns like square numbers on the diagonal or the symmetry between rows and columns. It's a handy visual reference for practicing times tables, checking homework, or building number sense beyond the standard 1–12 range.
